## Detailed Description

Magnet Station turns any magnet link in your browser into a one-click handoff to your Synology NAS. Click a magnet on any site and the torrent is added to Download Station instantly -- no copy/paste, no opening DSM, no third-party servers in the middle. The popup gives you a live view of active downloads with progress, lets you delete tasks, copy magnet URIs back out of the queue, and jump straight into the DSM web UI.

The extension works with any DSM 7.x NAS reachable on your network or via a QuickConnect ID. Your credentials and session live in the browser's own storage and are only ever sent to the Synology address you configured -- there is no analytics, no telemetry, and no remote backend operated by us. Light and dark mode are both supported, and the UI is fully localized through the standard extension i18n system.
